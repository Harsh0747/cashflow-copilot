# User Research Guide

## Interview objective

Understand how small businesses currently forecast cash, where the process fails, and which decisions create enough pain to support weekly use.

## Recruitment

Interview:

- 8 founders who directly manage cash
- 8 finance managers or accountants
- 4 fractional CFOs serving multiple SMEs

Prioritize service businesses with recurring payroll and invoice-based revenue.

## Questions

1. Walk me through the last time cash was tighter than expected.
2. What information did you use to decide what to pay or chase?
3. Show me the spreadsheet or report you use today.
4. Which inputs are hardest to keep current?
5. How far ahead do you genuinely trust your forecast?
6. Which overdue invoices deserve attention first, and how do you decide?
7. What happens after you identify a likely cash gap?
8. Which customer communications require approval?
9. What financial data would you refuse to upload?
10. What would make you return to this product every week?

## Concept test

Give the participant the sample prototype and ask them to:

1. Explain the projected cash position in their own words.
2. Identify the most urgent invoice.
3. Change assumptions for a 15% revenue decline.
4. Draft a reminder.
5. Describe what they would do next outside the product.

## Evidence thresholds

Proceed to private beta when:

- At least 12 of 20 participants experience the problem monthly or more often.
- At least 8 participants currently maintain a manual forecast.
- At least 5 pilot businesses use the product in four consecutive weeks.
- At least 70% of reviewed action recommendations are judged useful.
